Conversion
Hit frames are selected from authored cells1,2,3,5,7,10; grenade bursts use
cells1,2,3,4,6,8,11,14. Index0 is the author's empty leading cell.
All cells use a common center/pivot rather than independently shifting crops.
Hit output32x32 uses documented cool channel remapping; burst56x56 retains
the author's warm colors. Alpha below8 becomes the key; otherwise authored RGB is preserved and
alpha becomes binary255, retaining angular rays for global hardware Alpha. No animation frames are AI generated.
Beam samples the source red-laser profile, applies magenta tint and a13px
bright collision core. Procedural 24px vertical modulation repeats exactly.
The native grenade/medical sprites are new small text palette grids, not
modifications of downloaded artwork. Full recipes and hashes: sources.json
and assets/manifest.json source records.
